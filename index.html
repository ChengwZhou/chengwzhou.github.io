About

I am a Ph.D. student in the Department of Electrical, Computer, and Systems Engineering at Case Western Reserve University, advised by Prof. Gourav Datta. My research focuses on efficient and adaptive AI, spanning language models, vision architectures, and resource-constrained intelligent systems.

Edge & Near-Sensor Intelligence

Hardware-aware perception and energy- and bandwidth-efficient machine learning.
